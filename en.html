Nene Akagi

Inner Vision Photography
Photographer・Researcher

Photography that visualizes the inner world.
Through photography, I bring emotions and memories from within into visible form,
creating opportunities to encounter states of mind that may have gone unnoticed.

The act of looking at a photograph itself
becomes a process that leads to a deeper understanding of the inner self.

Through this practice, I explore the space between art and psychology
as a form of expression that supports self-understanding
and independent stress management.

Project

Inner Vision Photography is a project that visualizes inner experiences,
such as emotions and memories, through photography,
creating an experience for understanding oneself.

Through exhibitions, workshops, individual sessions, and research activities,
this project brings the intersection of art and psychology into society.

With future collaboration in medical care, welfare, education, and business in mind,
it proposes a new approach to helping individuals understand and regulate themselves
under the theme of independent stress management.
